# NOSTOS-0 pristine PSHG local-orientation transfer protocol

Protocol version: `nostos-pshg-external-orientation/1.0`

Frozen: 26 August 2026, before calculation of any NOSTOS-versus-orientation outcome in PSHG-TISS.

## Scientific question

Can the previously declared sigma-2 NOSTOS local structure-tensor field recover collagen direction in a new human-tissue acquisition when the reference direction is derived independently from polarization-resolved SHG physics rather than from the same grayscale image?

## External archive and cohort

The untouched confirmation archive is PSHG-TISS (`OSF.IO/UDTQP`; Hristu et al., *Scientific Data* 2022). It contains 512 × 512-pixel PSHG stacks acquired with a Leica TCS SP system over 125 × 125 µm fields. The primary cohort comprises every available unstained skin ROI and forward-SHG stack. An ROI is the inferential group. Files are selected by archive structure and filename only; no outcome-dependent exclusion is permitted.

## Input and independent reference

The NOSTOS input is the pixelwise arithmetic mean of all ten archived FSHG frames acquired from 0° through 180° in 20° increments. The reference is the archived `FI.tif` in-plane collagen-orientation map generated by the depositors' Fourier polarization-SHG model. Reference eligibility requires finite FI, `R2 >= 0.90`, `SNR >= 3 dB`, mean FSHG intensity greater than zero and an eight-pixel image-edge exclusion. No FI, R2 or SNR value enters the NOSTOS estimator.

The archived FI convention spans -90° to 90° and is treated as axial. NOSTOS direction is also axial. Error is the minimum angular difference modulo 180°.

## Frozen estimator and comparators

- Primary: NOSTOS local structure tensor at sigma 2 pixels (0.48828125 µm at the declared archive spacing), without adaptive scale selection or confidence filtering.
- Fixed-scale comparator: identical structure tensor at sigma 4 pixels.
- Conventional comparator: Gaussian-smoothed finite-difference gradient at sigma 2 pixels, rotated by 90° to represent line direction.

The implementation and signs may be checked on analytic gratings only. They may not be altered after PSHG outcomes are calculated.

## Statistics

The primary endpoint is pooled-pixel median axial error. A 10,000-draw bootstrap (seed 7,242,322) resamples whole ROIs and retains all eligible pixels within each selected ROI. Secondary endpoints are the pooled 75th percentile, the median of ROI-level median errors and axial alignment, defined as the mean cosine of twice the axial error. Results are also reported separately by archived skin-region class when available, without classwise significance claims.

## Prespecified success gates

All gates must pass:

1. at least 30 eligible ROIs;
2. at least 50,000 eligible reference pixels;
3. pooled median axial error no greater than 15°;
4. ROI-bootstrap upper 95% limit for median error no greater than 15°;
5. pooled 75th-percentile error no greater than 30°;
6. median ROI-level median error no greater than 15°;
7. axial alignment at least 0.65;
8. primary median error no worse than sigma 4 by more than 2°;
9. primary median error no worse than the smoothed-gradient comparator by more than 2°.

Failure of any gate is retained as the formal result. Passing validates a scale-declared local direction field in one pristine external acquisition and three human skin regions. It does not establish molecular orientation, cross-instrument universality, tissue mechanism, diagnosis or clinical use.
